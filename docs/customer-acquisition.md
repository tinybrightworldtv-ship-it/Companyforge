# Customer Acquisition Engine

CompanyForge should not assume that finding customers means buying ads. It builds a channel plan from the company's business model, ideal customer, problem, offer, geography, buying intent, trend evidence and available budget.

## Target categories

The engine can classify a company into:
1. Consumer / B2C
2. D2C / ecommerce
3. B2B
4. Enterprise
5. Local service
6. Marketplace
7. Creator / media
8. Developer / technical
9. Education
10. Health / wellness
11. Finance
12. Nonprofit / public service

It then chooses target dimensions such as geography, demographics, firmographics, job role, industry, company size, budget/income, interests, behaviors, pain points, use case, search intent, purchase intent, lifecycle, technology stack, community, creator affinity and language.

## Organic and free distribution

CompanyForge can plan:
- Instagram
- Facebook
- TikTok
- YouTube
- LinkedIn
- Pinterest
- X
- Threads
- opt-in WhatsApp
- opt-in Telegram
- Reddit and relevant communities
- search/SEO
- Google Business Profile for eligible businesses
- Bing Places
- Product Hunt
- free-permitted business/product directories
- partnerships, referrals and co-marketing
- permission-based email

Free means no media spend; it does not guarantee customers. The system must not automate spam, fake reviews, deceptive engagement or unsolicited bulk messaging.

## Paid advertising centers

The provider registry supports:
- Meta Ads
- Google Ads
- TikTok Ads
- LinkedIn Ads
- Pinterest Ads
- Microsoft Advertising
- Reddit Ads
- X Ads
- Snap Ads

Each provider is credential-dependent. CompanyForge keeps credentials server-side and treats external publishing and paid advertising as approval-gated actions.

## Social publishing workflow

For an approved offer, CompanyForge creates platform-specific:
- hook
- primary copy
- visual/video brief
- CTA
- destination
- UTM tracking
- compliance/community notes

A post is not considered published until the connected platform returns provider evidence.

## Trend-driven acquisition

Trend signals may come from Google Trends/search interest, social trends, search intent, marketplace signals, news/events or internal performance. Every signal carries source, geography, timestamp, evidence and confidence. A spike is a hypothesis, not proof of durable demand.

## Acquisition loop

Business idea → target category → buyer/user → target dimensions → trend discovery → customer problem → offer → channel selection → content/ad creation → approval → distribution → attribution → conversion measurement → experiment → learning → next action.

## Attribution

Campaigns use UTM parameters and conversion events including page views, signups, leads, checkout starts, purchases, subscriptions and qualified leads. Acquisition events are persisted so CompanyForge can compare channels and improve future decisions.

## Evidence rule

CompanyForge must distinguish planned, approved, submitted and active states. It cannot claim that an advertisement, social post, listing or customer acquisition action actually happened unless the external provider or owned system supplies evidence.
