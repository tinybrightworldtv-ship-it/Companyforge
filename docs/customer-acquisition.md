# Customer Acquisition Engine

CompanyForge should not assume that "finding customers" means buying ads. It builds a channel plan from the company's ideal customer, problem, offer, geography, buying intent and available budget.

## Distribution layers

1. **Organic social:** Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest and X where relevant.
2. **Community discovery:** Reddit and other communities only where their rules permit useful participation.
3. **Search:** SEO pages, problem-specific guides, comparisons, demos and other intent-driven content.
4. **Listings:** eligible local/business directories and Google Business Profile for qualifying storefront or service-area businesses.
5. **Partnerships:** complementary businesses, creators, affiliates and referral relationships.
6. **Paid acquisition:** Meta Ads and other paid networks only after the offer and landing page have measurable conversion evidence.

## Social sharing workflow

For each approved offer, CompanyForge creates platform-specific assets rather than cross-posting identical copy:
- hook
- primary copy
- visual/video brief
- CTA
- destination URL
- tracking parameters
- publishing time/window
- compliance/community notes

A post is not considered published until the connected platform reports success.

## Meta advertising

Meta advertising is treated as a paid execution path. CompanyForge can plan campaigns, audiences, creative variants, budgets, tracking and experiments, but must not claim an ad ran without provider evidence. Meta Ads Manager supports Facebook/Instagram placements, including Reels.

## Free distribution

"Free" means no media spend, not guaranteed customers. CompanyForge should prioritize legitimate zero-budget channels:
- organic social posts and short videos
- search/SEO
- eligible Google Business Profile listings
- relevant communities
- product/business directories that allow free listings
- partnerships and referrals

It must not automate spam, fake reviews, mass unsolicited messages, deceptive engagement or posting that violates platform/community rules.

## Customer discovery

The acquisition agent should discover:
- where the target customer already searches
- communities where the problem is discussed
- keywords and questions showing buying intent
- competitor acquisition patterns
- creators/partners with overlapping audiences
- suitable free listing opportunities
- paid channels worth testing

Every recommendation should include evidence, expected audience, execution method, cost, policy constraints and a measurement plan.
