# Trend Intelligence & Adaptive Targeting

CompanyForge treats trends as signals, not guarantees. It can combine trend sources with the company's business idea, customer profile, geography, seasonality, competition and internal performance.

## Trend sources
- Google Trends/search interest
- social trend signals
- search-intent signals
- marketplace signals
- news/event signals
- the company's own conversion and engagement data

Google Trends provides current search-interest and Trending Now views, including geography-specific views. CompanyForge should use trend observations with timestamps and evidence rather than claiming a trend is universal.

## Adaptive target categories

### 1. B2C / consumer
Target with pain points, interests, behaviors, demographics, geography and purchase intent.

### 2. D2C / ecommerce
Target product intent, search intent, interests, behaviors, geography and customer lifecycle.

### 3. B2B
Target industry, company size, job role, pain point, budget, technology and search intent.

### 4. Enterprise
Target industry, company size, decision-making roles, procurement context, technology stack, budget and business pain.

### 5. Local services
Target geography, local search intent, service need, urgency, lifecycle and purchase intent.

### 6. Marketplaces
Target both sides separately: supply/provider audience and buyer/customer audience, then model the network interaction.

### 7. Creator/media businesses
Target interests, communities, creator affinity, language, geography and content consumption behavior.

### 8. Developer/technical products
Target technology stack, developer role, technical communities, search intent and use cases.

### 9. Education
Target learner/student, parent/guardian, institution or employer depending on who pays and who uses.

### 10. Health/wellness
Target use case, lifecycle, geography, interests and purchase intent while respecting applicable advertising and privacy rules.

### 11. Finance
Target use case, lifecycle, geography, budget/income signals and purchase intent with financial-ad compliance controls.

### 12. Nonprofit/public-service
Target geography, need/use case, language, lifecycle and relevant communities.

## Targeting dimensions
CompanyForge can combine:
- geography
- demographics
- firmographics
- job role
- industry
- company size
- budget/income where appropriate and legally permitted
- interests
- behaviors
- pain points
- use case
- search intent
- purchase intent
- lifecycle
- technology stack
- communities
- creator affinity
- language

## Decision flow

Business idea → classify business model → identify buyer/user → identify target dimensions → discover trends → map trends to customer problems → create offers/content → distribute → measure conversion → update targeting.

The system must not assume that a trending topic means commercial demand. Every trend opportunity should carry evidence, timestamp, confidence and a validation experiment.
